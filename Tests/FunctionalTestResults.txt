UI test

All six synthetic interaction checks passed while the audio engine ran:

Scope and limitations
---------------------
These results verify offline processing and in-process synthetic UI events.
They do not verify audible speaker output, hardware-device routing/latency,
native standalone-window responsiveness, or real mouse/keyboard delivery.
Native UI automation was blocked by macOS Accessibility permissions.
Quick mode skips the optional full demo and listening-guide renders.

Grain Field built-in sounds (0.4)

Most sounds start from instruments recorded by the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL),
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Each was then slowed
and smeared (Paulstretch-style), filtered and worn, so it no longer sounds like the instrument.

  glass haze    rubbed wine glasses, stretched 4x, with a faint octave shimmer
  tape choir    Renaissance organ through vowel-like resonances, stretched, with tape wow
  dust halo     vibraphone played backwards and stretched, with record dust
  bowed air     bowed psaltery, stretched, with breath around it
  low hum       bowed vibraphone, stretched long and kept dark
  glass drone   the wine glasses again, stretched much further
  ocean drum    VCSL ocean drum, filtered and looped

Synthesised (no recordings): low room, rain, wind.

Every pitched sound keeps a note map (Source/BuiltinSounds.inc), so grains can be tuned to the chord.

To rebuild: Tools/select_samples.py picks the files from a VCSL checkout, Tools/build_textures.py makes
the Ogg files here and Source/BuiltinSounds.inc. Defaults are relative to the repository; use --vcsl
to select a different VCSL checkout. Scratch files are written to build/soundbuild.
